\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Exploitation and objective mismatch in model-based learning.}
The failure modes we measure build on two foundational observations. First, \citet{ha2018worldmodels} showed that controllers exploit deterministic or low-temperature learned environments, motivating temperature scaling, ensembles, and pessimistic model-based RL \citep{kurutach2018metrpo,chua2018pets,janner2019mbpo,yu2020mopo,kidambi2020morel}. Second, \citet{lambert2020objective} documented the mismatch between predictive accuracy and control performance, motivating value-equivalent world models \citep{farahmand2017vaml,grimm2020valueequivalence} and joint-embedding planners \citep{maes2026leworldmodel,singh2026evalprotocol,cheung2026onestep}. Recent theory proves any imperfect dynamics model remains exploitable over rich policy classes \citep{bhamidipaty2026exploitable}, while our coverage retraining tests one targeted data-aggregation step \citep{ross2012agnostic}.

\paragraph{Evaluating world models as judges and arenas.}
Recent benchmarks evaluate generative video and dynamics models as zero-shot policy judges \citep{quevedo2025worldgym,guo2026ctrlworld,geminirobotics2025veo,valevski2025gamengen,bruce2024genie}. Strong correlations across fixed policy pools align with our unoptimised $B ={}$\Bp{0} regime before active search widens ground-truth shortfall. \citet{ariq2026ravel} study top-one selection regret on fixed pools via the optimiser's curse \citep{smith2006optimizer}, while \citet{yao2026rolloutfaith} and \citet{vakalis2026intervention} apply interventional rollout diagnostics. Relative to prior work, our contribution pairs controlled pipeline measurement against a fresh-seed exact-simulator reference (distinguishing bad-plan rejection from good-plan discovery) with perturbation-controlled subsystem patching and pre-training audit evidence across a \Ladder-checkpoint ladder (extended review in Appendix~\ref{app:morerelated}).

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Practical guidance.}\label{sec:whattodo}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Audit two-sided task-horizon verdicts on logged trajectories before optimising.} Scoring one fixed logged plan per starting instance at the full horizon (reusing simulator replays across models) ranks PPO exploitation within our model ladder comparably to \Bp{3}-plan search, with no statistically established difference (\AcEOneWithin{} vs.\ \AcWithin{} within family; \AcEOneRho{} vs.\ \AcRho{} pooled). Tracking single-plan false rejection alongside false acceptance (\ExtTrRandOneAllFalseRej{} at \ExtTrEpochs{} epochs vs.\ \ExtTrMainRandOneAllFalseRej{} at two epochs) prevents mistaking zero-acceptance pessimistic collapse for calibration.
\item \textbf{Pair learned-model evaluations against a stochasticity reference.} Even an exact simulator incurs \RandThouPerfHack{} false acceptance at $B ={}$\Bp{3} (\RandOnePerfHack{} at \Bp{0}) from finite-sample luck. Without a reference, selection variance masquerades as model exploitation.
\item \textbf{Track ground-truth discovery alongside false acceptance.} Screening a planner's final choice on the true simulator removes false acceptance yet recovers none of the \PaTenThouShort{} lost ground-truth success\RpWhatClause. Meanwhile, models discard \RandThouFalseRej{} of truly passing choices versus \RandThouPerfFalseRej{} for the reference.
\item \textbf{Target structural bottlenecks rather than generic conservatism.} Variance penalties, halts, and shrinkage trade false acceptance directly against false rejection. Coverage augmentation repairs fiscal covariate gaps (\emph{treasury}, \emph{recover}) yet leaves topological grid blind spots (\emph{power}) intact.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Scope and Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{One-step training and spatial drift.}
Our ladder's full-data one-step models train for \HpSamplesOs{} examples (two epochs over \TrainTransitions{} transitions, under two H200-equivalent GPU hours per run), where held-out log-likelihood is still improving while all \HlSmallN{} small-data models already overfit. By month \Horizon{}, the primary model equals or exceeds start-state copying error on \HorRestN{} of \HorOverlays{} spatial overlays (\HorLadGeMin{}--\HorLadGeMax{} across the ladder; Appendix~\ref{app:ladderfid}). Extending primary one-step training sixfold on a single seed to \ExtTrEpochs{} epochs lifts held-out ELBO by \ExtTrTestElboGain{} nats and improves \emph{treasury} PPO. Yet it worsens \Horizon-month spatial drift even on training cities, flipping miscalibration to zero-acceptance rejection on \ExtTrZeroAccFams{} task families while leaving a \ExtTrTenThouShort{} shortfall at \Bp{4} (Section~\ref{sec:extended}; Appendix~\ref{app:extended}). Much of the open-loop failure we measure therefore reflects exposure bias in models whose task-horizon spatial rollouts have degraded. Achieving high \Horizon-month spatial fidelity via broader transition diversity, multi-step stochastic objectives, or architectures with explicit graph routing and long-context memory (and testing single-plan calibration in that regime) remains the highest-priority extension.

\paragraph{Single architecture family and domain.}
Our cross-model correlation spans \AcClusters{} closely related ConvNeXt U-Net checkpoints in one spatial-economic engine, so generalisation to stronger models, new architectural families, or external domains remains unestablished. Appendix~\ref{app:external}'s TD-MPC2 experiment is strictly a software check outside TD-MPC2's native \ExtMppiHorizon-step closed-loop MPC horizon. Neural checkpoint weights are withheld pending clarity on GPL derivative-work status, limiting off-the-shelf model reuse even though the bit-exact engine, tasks, data generator, and \hpd{} harness are released in full.

\paragraph{Planning horizons and closed-loop control.}
Regarding planning horizons, \RpOpenLoopLead{} over \Horizon{} months. Closed-loop execution \RpSees{} less drift (PPO passes \RlClosed{} closed loop vs.\ \RlOracle{} open loop), and \RpLimitsText. High-frequency short-horizon MPC with learned value functions \citep{jiang2015horizon,hansen2024tdmpc2} and top-$k$ engine screening remain natural extensions.

\paragraph{Task difficulty bars, policy maturity, and ensemble design.}
Task thresholds sit midway between no-build policies and engine search. \ThresholdSentence{} At \RlMonths{} steps, engine-trained PPO passes \RlEngFull{} of instances and is still climbing, characterising early-to-mid policy learning, and our \DefMembers-member seed ensemble omits bootstrap data resampling.

\paragraph{Sample precision and pipeline decomposition.}
Precision is bounded by cluster and seed counts (\HeldOutFamilies{} core and \AllCities{} total cities, \PaInstances{} instances at \Bp{4}, \DefInstances{} defence instances, one coverage-retrained model seed, and \CovRlSeeds{} PPO seeds per world-model cell vs.\ \RlEngSeeds{} on the engine). Because learned-model and exact-simulator searches generally select different candidate plans, our reference benchmarks operational pipeline discrepancy rather than uniquely decomposing failures into directional bias, rollout variance, and altered plan rankings.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}\label{sec:conclusion}
Pairing learned world models against a bit-exact spatial-economic simulator shows how short-horizon fidelity gates fail to prevent long-horizon planning failures when coupled accounting, delayed fiscal settlements, and discrete network cutoffs interact. Under active search, model blind spots stall ground-truth progress as compute grows, while post-hoc regularisers cut false acceptance only by discarding genuine solutions. Within our tested model ladder, however, downstream RL exploitation is rankable before policy training begins by checking two-sided task verdicts on one logged plan per starting instance across a held-out city bank. Together, these findings show the value of exact pipeline references and horizon-matched calibration before heavy policy optimisation.
